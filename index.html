What I build

Most of the range below runs from no backend at all to a full one, on purpose. The tool should match the job, not the other way round.

Products with real backends

Boardly and Victorious Concept both run on Postgres through Supabase, with actual payment infrastructure including webhooks, refunds, and payouts.

Systems clients can run themselves

The teaching portfolio hands editorial control to a non technical client through a CMS, rather than routing every content change back through me.

Full sites with no framework at all

Stride and Fernhollow are plain HTML and hand written JavaScript, with a full shopping flow and a full booking flow and no backend or build step.

Real client work, start to finish

First Experts Logistics and ShelemJ Resources are live for paying businesses right now, built, launched, and still maintained.
