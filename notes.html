Not a generic blog. Field notes on the actual projects on this site, the move from lab science into code, and what worked when I had no clients yet.

6 min read From the Lab Bench to the Browser Career

For four years, my working life had nothing to do with code. I was in hospital laboratories, running biochemical assays, calibrating equipment, and writing up results that other people would use to make real decisions about someone's health. There was no room for "close enough." A result was either accurate or it wasn't, and if it wasn't, someone downstream would be working from bad information.
6 min read Getting My First Freelance Client in Lagos Career

When I finished my frontend training in January 2026, I had a certificate, two practice projects built during the course, and absolutely nothing else. No agency behind me, no network of developer friends passing along work, no portfolio anyone outside my own head had ever heard of. Just a laptop and a plan to figure it out.

6 min read What's Actually Running Behind Boardly Behind the scenes

I wanted to write down what's actually running back there, partly because it's the part that never shows up in a screenshot.
7 min read The Medical Side I Don't Always Lead With Medicine and Science

Most people who find this site land on the code first: the projects, the stack, the deployed sites. What tends to get less airtime is the four years before any of that, years spent in hospital laboratories and inside a state health insurance agency, doing work that had nothing to do with a browser. I want to write it down properly, not as a footnote to the developer story, but on its own terms.
