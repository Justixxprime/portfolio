Victorious Concept

A production e commerce platform for a real, currently trading business, built to actually process payments rather than simulate them.

01

The client

Victorious Concept sells bags, shoes, clothing, and slippers, and is a real business run by a family member, still trading today. An earlier training project, built in 2025 before this platform existed, is kept live on a separate URL as an honest record of where the work started rather than replaced or hidden.

02

The product

Built in React 19 and Vite, with Tailwind 4 for styling, Framer Motion for interface animation, and Supabase for the product catalog and order data. The site covers a full shop with categories and collections, cart, wishlist, checkout, order history, order tracking, an admin panel, source requests for products not yet in stock, a lookbook, and a journal.

03

The system

How an order actually gets confirmed as paid, not just submitted.

04

The payment infrastructure

This is the part that separates it from a typical portfolio storefront.

Real refunds

Real Vercel serverless functions handle the order lifecycle directly: create-order.js creates the order record, paystack-webhook.js confirms payment came through Paystack before the order is treated as paid, and process-refund.js handles a genuine refund back through the same gateway. Contact and newsletter submissions go through their own dedicated functions rather than a shared catch all endpoint.

A checkout button that looks complete but trusts the browser to confirm payment is not actually secure. The webhook exists so that Paystack, not the customer's browser, is the source of truth for whether money actually moved. That distinction is what makes this a real payment system.

05

The result

The platform is still in active production for the client, with a documented internal production audit tracking what still needs attention as real orders come through.

WHY PAYSTACK, NOT STRIPE
Requirementaccept real Naira payments from Nigerian customers with local card and bank transfer support
Constraintthe client's actual customers are in Nigeria, and Stripe's Nigerian support is weaker than a Nigeria first processor
DecisionPaystack, with a webhook as the source of truth for payment confirmation
Consequencebetter local payment method coverage, at the cost of a smaller ecosystem of prebuilt integrations than Stripe offers

The webhook detail matters more than the processor choice itself. Confirming payment from Paystack's server side callback, not from the customer's browser, is what makes this a real payment system rather than one that trusts the client to report its own success.
