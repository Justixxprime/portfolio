Case study
E commerce

Victorious Concept

A production e commerce platform for a real, currently trading business, built to actually process payments rather than simulate them.

The client

Victorious Concept sells bags, shoes, clothing, and slippers, and is a real business run by a family member, still trading today. An earlier training project, built in 2025 before this platform existed, is kept live on a separate URL as an honest record of where the work started rather than replaced or hidden.

The product

Built in React 19 and Vite, with Tailwind 4 for styling, Framer Motion for interface animation, and Supabase for the product catalog and order data. The site covers a full shop with categories and collections, cart, wishlist, checkout, order history, order tracking, an admin panel, source requests for products not yet in stock, a lookbook, and a journal.

The payment infrastructure

This is the part that separates it from a typical portfolio storefront. Real Vercel serverless functions handle the order lifecycle directly: create-order.js creates the order record, paystack-webhook.js confirms payment came through Paystack before the order is treated as paid, and process-refund.js handles a genuine refund back through the same gateway. Contact and newsletter submissions go through their own dedicated functions rather than a shared catch all endpoint.

Why the webhook matters

A checkout button that looks complete but trusts the browser to confirm payment is not actually secure. The webhook exists so that Paystack, not the customer's browser, is the source of truth for whether money actually moved. That distinction is what makes this a real payment system.

Ongoing

The platform is still in active production for the client, with a documented internal production audit tracking what still needs attention as real orders come through.
