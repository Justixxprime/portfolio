Case study
E commerce concept

Stride

A full sneaker shopping experience with no framework, no build step, and no backend at all, proving the front end alone can carry real weight.

stride-styles.obiomachibuezej.workers.dev

The constraint

Stride was built deliberately without React, without a build tool, and without a server. Plain HTML, Tailwind pulled in through a CDN link, and hand written JavaScript carry the entire experience. Every order and every saved wishlist item lives in the browser's own local storage, which is exactly what makes the site safe to demo without a real payment system behind it.

What that still had to cover

A full catalog of 22 products across running, trail, lifestyle, and casual categories, with working filters, sort, and search. A Build Your Own configurator for custom sneakers. A three step checkout wizard. A full page cart alongside a quicker drawer version. A wishlist. A size quiz. A side by side comparison tool. Product photography falls back gracefully through several formats, and any product missing a real photo shows a tinted placeholder rather than a broken image.

Why this project matters on this site

Most of the other flagship work here leans on Supabase or Vercel functions to do the heavy lifting. Stride proves that is a choice, not a limitation: the same interaction quality, the same completeness of flow, holds up with nothing but vanilla JavaScript underneath it.

What I would change today: the local storage cart does not survive a browser data clear, which is fine for a portfolio demo but would need a real backend before this could hold actual customer orders.

WHY NO FRAMEWORK, ON PURPOSE
Requirementprove that a complete e commerce interaction flow does not require React or a build tool to feel real
ConstraintReact would have made the same result faster to build, which would have undermined the actual point of the exercise
Decisionplain HTML, Tailwind CDN, and hand written JavaScript, deliberately
Consequencemore manual DOM manipulation than a framework would require, in exchange for concrete proof that the fundamentals hold up without one

This is the one project on the site where the constraint was chosen on purpose rather than dictated by the client or the timeline. It exists specifically to answer a question about my own fundamentals, not to solve a business problem.
