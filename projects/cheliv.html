Cheliv Compassionate Care

A home healthcare provider needed a site that felt trustworthy and easy to navigate for people who might be stressed or in a hurry. Built during frontend training, 2025.

The brief

A home healthcare provider needed a marketing site that did one thing well: reassure a visitor who is already stressed, rather than add to that stress with a confusing layout or a wall of clinical text.

What it covers

Service pages and disease management program listings, written to explain what the provider does without overwhelming someone who is looking for help for a family member. A working request information form connects visitors directly to the care team, so the site does more than describe services, it starts the actual conversation.

Where this sits in the timeline

This was built while I was still in frontend training, alongside the E Commerce Showcase site, before I completed that program in January 2026. Everything else on this site was built after. The same attention to detail I picked up running lab assays shows up in how closely I checked this one before calling it done.
